TTS Personal Assistant — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

TTS Personal Assistant is operated by Ridhwan Essop, trading as The Technology Simplifier, in Johannesburg, South Africa. It is a personal WhatsApp assistant currently being configured and tested. Scheduled reminders and task updates are not currently active.

Go to data deletion instructions

Information we collect

When you message the assistant, we may store your WhatsApp phone number, message content and interactions, message identifiers, message types, timestamps, the receiving business phone number identifier, and associated message metadata.

For outgoing replies, we may store the recipient phone number, reply text, sending attempts, API acceptance identifiers, and errors needed to troubleshoot delivery.

How we use information

We use this information to receive and respond to messages, test the connection, prevent duplicate processing, and investigate technical problems.

The current test configuration only sends automatic replies to the configured owner's phone number. Messages received from other people may still be stored if delivered to the assistant's webhook.

Service providers and sharing

Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform provides messaging. Supabase provides the assistant's database and backend services. These providers may process information outside South Africa under their own terms and privacy policies.

This public policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about visits, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. WhatsApp messages are not published on this page or in its public source repository.

The operator does not sell message information or use it for advertising. The current assistant configuration does not send messages to an external generative AI service.

Storage and security

Access to the assistant's backend and administrative tools is restricted. Credentials are stored separately from public page code. No security measure can guarantee complete protection against every risk.

Retention

During the current testing stage, message and reply records remain stored until manually deleted. Automatic deletion based on the age of a record is not currently configured. Service provider logs and backups may have separate retention periods.

Data deletion instructions

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of information held by TTS Personal Assistant.

  1. Email ridhwanessop@gmail.com .
  2. Use the subject: TTS Personal Assistant data request.
  3. Include your WhatsApp phone number with its country code and describe the information you want deleted, accessed, or corrected.

Requests are handled manually. We may ask you to verify control of the relevant WhatsApp number before acting. Do not send passwords, access tokens, verification codes, or identity documents with your initial request.

For a verified deletion request, we will remove the associated message and outgoing reply records from the assistant's active database, unless retention is required by law, and confirm the outcome by email.

Deleting assistant records does not delete messages from your WhatsApp application, other people's devices, or information independently retained by Meta. Copies in provider backups or logs may remain until the relevant retention period expires.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the assistant's operation or handling of information changes. The date above identifies the latest update.
